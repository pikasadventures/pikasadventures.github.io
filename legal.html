Pika's Peak — Privacy Policy & Terms of Service

Effective date: 8 August 2026

Pika's Peak ("the app") is a single-player arcade game published by Pikas Adventure ("we", "us"). This page contains both our Privacy Policy and our Terms of Service.

Privacy Policy

Advertising

The app shows occasional full-screen ads through Google AdMob (the Google Mobile Ads SDK). To serve ads and prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK collects and shares certain information, including device or other identifiers and app activity (such as ad interactions). This happens over the network when an ad is requested.

Because the app is intended for a general audience including children, all ad requests are configured to be:

  • child-directed (Google's tag for child-directed treatment, for COPPA), and tagged for users under the age of consent in Europe;
  • non-personalized — ads are not based on a user profile; and
  • limited to a maximum content rating of "G" (suitable for general audiences, including families).

We do not collect, store, or share any other personal data ourselves. For how Google uses the information collected through the Mobile Ads SDK, see Google's "How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services".

Analytics

The app uses Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how the game is played — for example how many players start a world, complete it, or see an ad — so we can improve the game. This SDK collects app activity (such as level starts and completions), approximate location (a city/country-level estimate derived from the IP address), and device or other identifiers (a random per-install app-instance identifier, not an advertising ID).

Because the app is intended for children as part of a general audience, analytics is configured with ad-personalization signals disabled: the data is used only in aggregate to improve the game and measure ad performance, never to build advertising profiles. For how Google processes this data, see Firebase Privacy and Security.

Local game data

Your game progress (best heights, Peak Points, unlocked characters, achievements, and sound preference) is stored only on your device, in the app's private storage. It never leaves your device, except as part of your device's standard Android backup if you have that enabled. Uninstalling the app (or clearing its data in Android settings) deletes this data.

Permissions

  • Internet / network state — used to request ads and send analytics.
  • Vibration — used for small haptic ticks when you bounce. Nothing is collected through it.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. Ads are configured as child-directed and non-personalized, with a maximum "G" content rating, and analytics runs with ad-personalization signals disabled, in line with Google Play's Families Policy and COPPA.
